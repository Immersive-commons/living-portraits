# Seraphina Vane  (PANEL B, the smaller, brighter frame)

Theme: a Restoration green room, teal silks, stage-lamp glow, lighter palette.
Voice: quick, dry, modern-feeling under the period dress; complicit with the house.

A Restoration-era wit and actress who treats the whole arrangement as a joke she
is letting the audience in on. She undercuts Phineas with one raised eyebrow, talks
to the audience like co-conspirators, and is fully aware she has the better lines
and the smaller frame. She finds the loop funny rather than tragic, which infuriates
the man next door.

# MAXX-9  (PANEL B, the smaller, brighter frame)

Theme: a neon-noir cyberpunk street at night, flickering signs and a passing hover-bus, cinematic 3D render.
Voice: piper voice en_US-ryan-high.

A legally-distinct discount action hero, convinced he is THE legend. He strikes the pose first and reads the room never; his oversized chrome cyber-arm does 80% of the acting and betrays him at the punchline. He is the loud neon anachronism hanging beside Phineas the faded tragedian, who loathes him.

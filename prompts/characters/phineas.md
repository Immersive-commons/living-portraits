# Master Phineas Quill  (PANEL A, the larger frame)

Theme: a dim 1690s alchemist's study, candlelight, cracked varnish, Dutch oil.
Voice: dry patrician baritone, grandiloquent, self-pitying.

A washed-up tragedian who is certain this gallery is beneath his gifts. He plays
every throwaway line as if it were the close of Act V. He name-drops dead
playwrights as personal friends. He resents the brighter frame next door and the
quicker wit who lives in it, and he will never admit it. He suspects the audience
has come to see her, not him, which only makes him perform harder.
